How this is made

  • Sources: , collected once a day at a low request rate after reviewing each site's robots.txt and terms of use. Only aggregates are published here: no job titles, companies or links.
  • What counts as Data/AI: keyword search on job boards is noisy (sales posts offering "data có sẵn" match "data"), so each posting is classified from its title and the board's own job category.
  • One ad, one count: the same ad on two boards is matched by normalized company name and title similarity and counted once.
  • Pipeline: Python ingestion → PostgreSQL → dbt (staging, intermediate, marts, 70 data tests) → this page, rebuilt by GitHub Actions every morning. Code: GitHub.
